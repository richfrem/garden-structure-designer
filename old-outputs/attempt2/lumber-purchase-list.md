# Materials & Lumber Order: Saanich Hexagon Gazebo (10ft Final)

**Project:** 10' Regular Hexagon Pavilion  
**Total Height:** 10.0' Limit  
**Lumber Grade:** Rough Sawn Western Red Cedar (Structural Grade)

---

## 1. Primary Structural Lumber

| Component | Dimensions | Qty | Order Length | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Posts** | 6x6 Rough Cedar | 6 | 10 Feet | Primary supports. Cut to 8' 5" (100" total). |
| **Ring Beams** | 6x6 Rough Cedar | 6 | 6 Feet | Perimeter beams. Cut to ~5' face segments. |
| **Hip Rafters** | 4x6 Rough Cedar | 6 | 6 Feet | Roof rafters. Cut to approx 5.4' (65"). |
| **Center Hub** | 8x8 Rough Cedar | 1 | 2-3 Feet | Compound hub. (Or laminate 2x8s). |

> [!TIP]
> **Consolidation Note:** If sourcing from a yard that charges by the board-foot, you can consolidate the 6x6 members:
> - Posts: 6 x 10' lengths.
> - Beams: 3 x 12' lengths (cut 2 segments per board).
