## Cost Optimisations

1. **Source rough-sawn cedar** from Slegg and dress only visible faces — saves ~20% vs fully-dressed.
2. **Rent clamps and augur bit** from a tool library (Victoria Tool Library) instead of buying.
3. **Skip sonotube** and hand-dig 12" holes if soil allows — saves $108.
4. **Finish pre-assembly** (easier access, less overspray, better coverage in joinery pockets).
5. **King post hub off-cut** — Slegg will often sell 4 ft of 6×6 as off-cut at scrap price.
