---

## Ordering Rule

> **Order to raw stock lengths, not cut lengths.** You trim to the cut list at the saw.  
> Add ~10% waste/defect allowance. Buy one full extra piece of each profile for test cuts.

## Consumables & Shop Supplies

- Circular saw blade: 10" combo, 40T (replace before bevel cuts)
- Belt sander belts: 80-grit × 6, 120-grit × 6
- Mortise chisel set: 1.5" and 3/4" bench chisels + mallet
- Layout tools: sliding bevel, 16" speed square, string lines × 6
- Clamps: Bar clamps 48" × 4 (for beam ring glue-up)
- Finish: Penofin Transparent Cedar or Sikkens Cetol 1 (2× coats, all surfaces pre-assembly)
